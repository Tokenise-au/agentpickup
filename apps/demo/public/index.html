01 / the gap

Agents can pay for real things. Nobody proved who collected them.

payx402 · MPP

Money moves per request, in USDC or by card through Stripe. Signed receipts prove a payment happened.

✕ no hand-over
shopUCP · ACP

An agent can choose a pickup store at checkout. Neither standard says how collection is proven.

✕ no hand-over
platformsordering apps

Pickup codes exist, but each is a closed app tied to its own accounts and saved cards.

✕ closed
hand overagentPickup

A signed pass the counter scans. It proves the right person collected, and a held card is captured on that scan.

✓ open

02 / how it works

From “get me a coffee” to the counter

01

Find

Someone asks their agent for a flat white near work. The agent searches the directory for shops near them that sell it, which are open now, and how each one takes payment.

find_shops · GET /directory/v1/shops?q=flat+white&lat=…&lng=…
02

Order

The agent orders from the shop's live menu. Prices always come from the menu, never from the request, and the order lands on the venue's Square screens like any other.

create_pickup_order · POST /api/orders
03

Pay

USDC over x402 is paid as the order is placed, and can be paid back if nobody collects. A card through Stripe's agent rails is held until the scan. Either way the money goes to the venue, not us.

402 Payment Required → x402 · MPP · Stripe
04

Ready

Staff mark it ready on Square or on our scanner. The customer gets a receipt when they pay, and the QR when the order is ready to collect.

order.ready · QR emailed · webhook signed
05

Collect

Staff scan the QR with any phone camera. The signature checks offline, the pass works once, and a held card is captured on that scan.

order.collected · sig ok · single use

04 / for builders

One server, every way to order

  • An MCP server. Any agent can find shops, order, pay, join a group order and fetch the pass. Ours is hosted at /mcp.
  • A fixed x402 address. Order and pay in one call, at a URL the x402 catalogues can list.
  • A public directory. Shops near someone that sell what they want, from here and from listed sites.
  • Every agent checkout. x402, MPP, UCP and ACP, and Stripe Checkout with the card held until pickup.
  • Safe by default. Prices come from the menu, claims are atomic so two scanners can't both hand over, and staff routes sit behind a key.

05 / the pass

An open pickup pass

A small signed token any facilitator, wallet or platform can issue and check. It carries no names, items or amounts, so a leaked screenshot gives nothing away.

  • Compact JWS, EdDSA. It fits in a QR a normal phone camera opens.
  • Checkable offline. Keys come from the shop's JWKS, so a locker or kiosk can check one with no network.
  • Next to x402's receipts. The receipt proves payment; the pass proves collection.
  • Fills UCP's gap. It's the proof of collection UCP's pickup method leaves open.

format v1 · draft · feedback welcome

06 / standards

Speaks every agent checkout

x402

USDC per order, with bazaar discovery, signed offers and receipts, retry-safe payment ids, and Sign-In-With-X so the paying wallet can fetch its pass again.

/api/x402/order
MPP

Stripe and Tempo's Machine Payments Protocol: card or Link through a Shared Payment Token, straight into the shop's Stripe.

/api/orders/:id/mpp
UCP

Google's Universal Commerce Protocol: checkout with the pickup method, and the pass as the order's link.

/.well-known/ucp
AP2

Every checkout is signed by the shop, and the platform's mandate proving the buyer approved those exact terms is checked and kept.

UCP mandates
ACP

OpenAI and Stripe's Agentic Commerce Protocol: checkout sessions with in-store pickup, delegated payment and signed webhooks.

/.well-known/acp.json
TAP

Visa's Trusted Agent Protocol, Mastercard Agent Pay and Web Bot Auth signatures, checked on every agent payment route.
