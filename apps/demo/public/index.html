/how

Five steps, one QR

The same flow for a coffee, a repaired phone or a parcel at the door.

#StepWhat happensUnder the hood
01Buy onlineOn your site, in a chat, or an AI agent calling your shop's API.POST /api/orders
02PayCard through your Stripe, held and not charged. Or USDC over x402, no sign-up needed.Stripe · x402
03Get the QRAn email from your address with a signed QR and a six-letter pickup code.pass.emailed
04ScanAny phone camera or USB scanner opens the hand-over screen. Nothing to install./staff
05Hand overThe hold is captured. If nobody collects, the hold is released automatically.order.collected

/use-cases

Anything handed over in person

cafes/

Cafés and restaurants

Order ahead, skip the queue. Pickup windows, an "it's ready" email, and an optional no-show fee.

retail/

Retail click and collect

Hold the card at order time and take the money at pickup, so an out-of-stock item never needs a refund.

delivery/

Couriers and delivery

The scan at the door is proof of delivery, and it releases the payment to the merchant.

events/

Events and experiences

Signed tickets that can't be forged, checked in at the door with a phone.

lockers/

Lockers and kiosks

Passes are signed, so a locker can check one against your public key with no network connection.

rentals/

Rentals and repairs

Hand over the bike, the tool or the repaired phone against a code only the paying customer has.

/developers

A library, not another app for your customers

Mount it in your server and keep your checkout, your domain and your brand.

  • Card and crypto. Stripe Checkout with the card held until pickup, or USDC over x402, so AI agents can buy without signing up.
  • Signed passes. Each QR is an Ed25519-signed token, good for exactly one pickup. Copied or altered codes are refused.
  • Your brand only. Emails, pass pages and the scanner use your name, colours and sending address.
  • Plugs into your stack. Signed webhooks for paid, ready, collected and expired orders. SQLite out of the box, or bring your own database.
  • Open source. Apache-2.0. Run it yourself for free.
server.ts
import { createCollect, SqliteStore, resendMailer } from "@agentpickup/server";

const pickup = createCollect({
  baseUrl: "https://orders.yourshop.com",
  merchant: {
    name: "Corner Café", brandColor: "#9a3412",
    fromEmail: "Corner Café <orders@yourshop.com>",
    location: { name: "Front counter" },
  },
  catalog,
  payments: {
    card: { secretKey: env.STRIPE_KEY, captureMode: "hold" },
    x402: { payTo: env.WALLET, network: "eip155:8453" },
  },
  store: new SqliteStore("./data/pickup.db"),
  mailer: resendMailer({ apiKey: env.RESEND_KEY }),
  signingKeyPem: env.PASS_SIGNING_KEY,
  staffKey: env.STAFF_KEY,
  onEvent: (e, order) => pos.push(e.type, order),
});

app.route("/", pickup.app);
