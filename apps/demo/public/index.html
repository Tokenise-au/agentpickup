Agents can pay.
Now they can pick up.

agentPickup turns a payment into a signed pickup pass. An agent pays over x402, or a person pays by card, and the buyer gets a QR. The merchant's scan proves the right person collected the order, and only then does the payment settle. It's an open format and a drop-in library for facilitators, agent wallets and ordering platforms.

/gap

Agents can pay for physical things.
Nobody can prove they collected them.

The payment and shopping standards each stop one step short of the counter.

LayerWho covers itWhat's therePickup proof
Payx402 · MPPMoney moves per request, in USDC or by card through Stripe. Signed receipts prove a payment happened.✕ none
ShopUCP · ACPUCP lets an agent choose a pickup store at checkout, and ACP has fulfilment options. Neither says how collection is proven.✕ none

/how

From 402 to hand-over

What happens on the wire, start to finish.

#StepWhat happensOn the wire
01QuoteThe agent creates an order and asks to pay. The price comes from the merchant's catalog, never from the request.402 Payment Required
02PayUSDC over x402 exact today. Holds through auth-capture as facilitators add it. Cards through Stripe are held until the scan.x402 · Stripe
03IssueThe pass is an Ed25519 JWS of about 250 bytes, emailed as a QR with a six-letter backup code. Public keys sit at /.well-known.pass.emailed
04VerifyAny phone camera or USB scanner reads it. The signature checks offline, and single use is enforced against the merchant's store.sig ok · single use
05SettleThe hold is captured on the scan, or released if nobody collects. A signed webhook goes to the platform.order.collected

/spec

An open pickup pass

A small, signed token that any facilitator, wallet or platform can issue and check. It carries no names, items or amounts, so a leaked screenshot gives nothing away.

  • Compact JWS, EdDSA. It fits in a QR that a normal phone camera opens.
  • Checkable offline. Keys come from the merchant's JWKS, so a locker or kiosk can verify one with no network.
  • Built for x402. It's designed to sit next to x402's offer-and-receipt extension: the receipt proves payment, and the pass proves collection.
  • Maps to UCP. The pass fills in the proof of collection that UCP's pickup fulfilment method leaves open.

/developers

One mount, the whole flow

Checkout, passes, scanner, emails and webhooks, on your domain and under your brand.

  • Every way agents pay. x402, MPP, UCP and ACP for agents, and Stripe Checkout with the card held until pickup.
  • An MCP server. @agentpickup/mcp lets any agent order, pay and fetch the pass. Try ours at /mcp.
  • Your brand only. Emails, pass pages and the scanner carry the merchant's name and colours, never ours.
  • Events you can act on. Signed webhooks for paid, ready, collected and expired orders.
  • Safe by default. Prices come from the catalog, claims are atomic so two scanners can't both hand over, and staff routes sit behind a key.
server.ts
import { createCollect, SqliteStore, resendMailer } from "@agentpickup/server";

const pickup = createCollect({
  baseUrl: "https://orders.yourshop.com",
  merchant: {
    name: "Corner Café", brandColor: "#9a3412",
    fromEmail: "Corner Café <orders@yourshop.com>",
    location: { name: "Front counter" },
  },
  catalog,
  payments: {
    x402: { payTo: env.WALLET, network: "eip155:8453" },
    card: { secretKey: env.STRIPE_KEY, captureMode: "hold" },
  },
  store: new SqliteStore("./data/pickup.db"),
  mailer: resendMailer({ apiKey: env.RESEND_KEY }),
  signingKeyPem: env.PASS_SIGNING_KEY,
  staffKey: env.STAFF_KEY,
  onEvent: (e, order) => platform.notify(e.type, order),
});

app.route("/", pickup.app);
