Support

Email hello@agentpickup.app. We usually reply within two business days, Brisbane time. For anything about an order, include its order number, which starts with ord_ and is on your receipt.

I ordered from a shop

  • Lost the QR? Open the link in your receipt or "ready" email ("Track my order" or "Open my pass"). The six-character pickup code on that page works at the counter too.
  • Got a receipt but no QR yet? Some shops send the QR when your order is ready. Your pass is at the "Track my order" link in the receipt meanwhile.
  • A problem with the order itself, such as a missing item or a refund? The shop handles that: reply to your receipt email and it goes to them.
  • Can't reach the shop, or think something's wrong with agentPickup itself? Email us.

I run a venue

  • To start taking agent orders, use the partner form or email us.
  • Orders from a connected Square POS show on your Square screens. Mark them ready in Square as usual, and scan the customer's QR to hand over.
  • To leave, email us and we'll remove your venue.

I'm building with it

  • Docs, code and issues are on GitHub.
  • Packages: @agentpickup/server, @agentpickup/core and @agentpickup/mcp.
  • Want your agent to pay from your own wallet? Set up an agent wallet.
  • Run agentPickup yourself, or take agent payments at a counter? List your shop in the directory: publish a listing on your site and register it on the connect page, or POST /directory/v1/listings with {"url": "…"}.
  • The MCP server for agents: https://agentpickup.app/mcp. Shops for agents to find: /directory/v1/shops.

Security

Found a security problem? Email hello@agentpickup.app before sharing it publicly, and we'll work with you on a fix.
