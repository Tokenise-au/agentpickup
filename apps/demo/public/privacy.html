Privacy

agentPickup is run by Tokenise Pty Ltd (ABN 56 691 028 038), Queensland, Australia. This page covers agentpickup.app: the shops hosted on it, the hosted MCP server at /mcp, the shop directory and the partner form. Businesses that run the agentPickup software on their own servers look after their own customers' information; ask them directly.

Questions or requests: hello@agentpickup.app.

What we collect, and why

WhenWhatWhy
You order from a shop hereYour email, your name if you give one, what you ordered, and when it was paid, made ready and collectedTo send your receipt and pickup QR, so staff know whose order it is, and so the shop can answer questions about it
You payThe payment reference. For USDC, the paying wallet address and the public transaction. Card details go to Stripe, never to usTo match the payment to your order and handle refunds
You or your AI agent use the MCP serverWhat the tools are asked to do: the shop, items and the email to send the QR toTo place and track the order
You fill in the partner formYour email, the kind of business and your noteTo reply to you
Staff use the scanner pageA cookie that keeps that device signed inSo staff don't sign in on every scan
You visit the sitePage counts from Cloudflare Web Analytics on the home page. It sets no cookies and doesn't follow you to other sites. Your IP address is held in memory for a minute to limit abuseTo see how many people visit, and to keep the service up

Shop staff see your name, what you ordered and your pickup code. They don't see your email. We don't sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who else handles it

  • The shop you ordered from, which is responsible for your order.
  • Square, when a shop is connected to it: the order with your name, so it shows on the shop's own screens. Not your email.
  • Railway hosts the site and stores orders.
  • Cloudflare runs the domain, sends our emails and counts page visits.
  • PayAI settles USDC payments. It sees the payment, not your email.
  • Stripe processes card payments for shops that take cards.

Some of these services store information outside Australia, including in the United States.

USDC payments are recorded on the Base blockchain, which is public. A blockchain record can't be changed or deleted by us or anyone else.

How long we keep it

Orders are kept while the shop runs on agentPickup, so the shop can answer questions and keep its records. Partner form entries are kept until you ask us to remove them. Payment records may need to be kept longer for tax and accounting.

Your choices

Email hello@agentpickup.app to see what we hold about you, correct it, or have your email and name removed from your orders. If you're not happy with how we've handled your information, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
