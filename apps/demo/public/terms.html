What agentPickup does

agentPickup lets shops take orders from people and from AI agents, emails the customer a pickup QR, and confirms the handover when staff scan it. The shop sells you the goods; we provide the technology. The shop is responsible for its menu, prices, the goods themselves and any refund.

Demo shops

The Demo Café and the Square Sandbox Café are demonstrations. Nothing is made or handed over. Agent payments to the Demo Café are made in real USDC by our own demo wallet, within daily limits. If you pay the Demo Café from your own wallet, email us and we'll send it back.

Ordering and paying

  • Your money goes straight to the shop: card payments to its Stripe account, USDC to its wallet. We never hold it.
  • A card payment may be held when you order and charged when you collect. If you don't collect in time, the shop's setting decides whether the hold is released or the payment refunded or kept.
  • USDC payments are final once they settle on the blockchain. Whether an uncollected order is paid back depends on how the shop has set up refunds.
  • Anyone holding your QR or pickup code can collect the order, so keep them to yourself, and collect before the time shown.

AI agents ordering for you

If your agent orders and pays on your behalf, you're responsible for what it orders, as if you'd done it yourself. Set a spending limit on any wallet it uses. Our MCP server tells agents to confirm the items and total with you before paying, but we can't control what other agents do.
